# How Po Works

<span class="audience">Part 1 · Conceptual Overview · For: anyone new to Po</span>

Po combines audio recording, on-device speech analysis, and AI coaching to turn everyday conversations into opportunities for improvement.

## What You Need

To use Po, users need an iPhone, the TestFlight app (Po is currently a beta app, so Apple delivers it through TestFlight instead of the App Store), a Po account, and an internet connection so Po can write feedback after each conversation.

## Recording and Identifying Conversations

Po listens only when the user taps the record button, and it stops when the user slides to stop. Users can record a meeting, a class, a phone call, or any other conversation they want feedback on. Po automatically separates a recording into individual conversations, filtering out long periods of silence and brief interactions that may not provide meaningful feedback.

During setup, Po learns to recognize the user's voice through a short voice sample. This allows Po to distinguish the user's speech from other speakers and focus its analysis on how the user communicates. By identifying which conversations are relevant, Po can provide more personalized and meaningful feedback based on the user's actual speaking habits.

Users remain in control of when Po listens. They can mute a recording, choose to mute recordings at night, or mark a conversation as personal when they want privacy.

## Turning Conversations into Feedback

After a conversation ends, Po analyzes how the user spoke and what they said. Po measures vocal characteristics such as speaking pace, pauses, tone, and word stress. Po also uses transcription and AI coaching to evaluate the conversation's content and identify areas for improvement.

Po considers the type of conversation when generating feedback. For example, explaining an idea in a meeting may require different communication skills from answering a question during an interview.

The resulting coaching report includes what went well, suggestions supported by examples from the user's own words, and speaking measurements. The report usually appears a minute or two after the conversation ends.

## Why a Conversation Might Not Get Feedback

- **It was too short.** A few words here and there aren't enough for useful feedback.
- **Po heard too little of the user's voice.** For example, the user mostly listened while others talked.
- **The user marked it as personal.** Po never coaches personal conversations.
