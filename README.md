# Po Speech Coach Documentation

<span class="audience">For: everyone</span>

Po is an iPhone app that coaches the way you speak. You record a real conversation, such as a meeting, a class, or an interview, and a minute or two later Po shows what went well and what to work on next time, quoting your own words.

This documentation is for **anyone who uses Po or is curious about it**. You don't need any technical background. If you can install an app on your iPhone, you can follow every page here.

> These pages describe **the newest version of Po**, including Practice. Po changes quickly while it is in beta, so a few screens on your phone might look slightly different.

## How This Documentation Is Organized

The documentation has two parts. Read Part 1 to understand Po, and use Part 2 when you want to get something done.

| Part | What it gives you | Start here |
|---|---|---|
| **Part 1 · Conceptual Overview** | What Po is, how it works, the speaking skills it looks at, what its scores mean, and how it handles your privacy. Read it once, before your first recording. | [What Is Po?](concept-what-is-po.md) |
| **Part 2 · Task Documentation** | Step-by-step instructions with screenshots: installing Po, setting it up, recording a talk, reviewing feedback, and keeping talks private. | [Overview of Tasks](task-index.md) |

## Where to Start

- **You haven't installed Po yet.** Read [What Is Po?](concept-what-is-po.md), then follow [Installing Po from TestFlight](task-install-po.md) and [Setting Up Po](task-set-up-po.md).
- **You have Po and want to record your first talk.** Go to [Recording a Talk](task-record-a-talk.md).
- **You recorded a talk and want to understand the feedback.** Read [Core Concepts](concept-core-concepts.md), then follow [Reviewing a Talk's Feedback](task-review-a-talk.md).
- **You want to know what Po keeps and sends.** Read [Privacy and Consent](concept-privacy-and-consent.md).

## How These Pages Are Written

- Names of buttons and labels are in **bold** and spelled exactly as they appear in Po. Po shows its main buttons in capital letters, so they appear here in capitals too, for example **CONTINUE**.
- Screenshots come from Po running on an iPhone simulator with made-up example talks. The people and meetings in them are not real.
- Use the search box at the top of the left menu to find a word on any page.
