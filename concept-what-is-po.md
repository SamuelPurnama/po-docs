# What Is Po?

<span class="audience">Part 1 · Conceptual Overview · For: anyone new to Po</span>

Po is an AI-powered speech coaching application for iPhone designed to help non-native English speakers improve their everyday communication skills. Unlike traditional speech coaching applications that require users to set aside time for practice sessions, Po is designed to learn from conversations that happen naturally throughout the day.

People communicate differently depending on the situation. Someone might need to explain their experience during a job interview, share an idea in a meeting, or tell a story to a friend. While speaking, it can be difficult to notice habits that affect how clearly a message comes across. Po helps users recognize these habits by analyzing their conversations and providing personalized feedback. Instead of offering only general speaking advice, Po identifies specific moments from conversations, quotes what the user said, and suggests what they could try differently. Users can also listen to recordings to hear those moments for themselves.

Po's longer-term goal is to identify speaking problems that occur repeatedly and turn them into personalized learning goals. This approach is intended to help users improve through the conversations they already have, rather than relying entirely on separate practice sessions.

## What Po Can and Cannot Do

### What Po Can Do

Po can:

- Record a conversation when the user taps the record button.
- Recognize the user's voice, so it gives feedback only on how the user speaks.
- Point out specific moments in a conversation and suggest a clearer way to say them.
- Show how the user's speaking changes over time.

### What Po Cannot Do

Po cannot:

- **Listen on its own.** Po records only when the user starts a recording.
- **Check grammar.** Po focuses on how clearly a message comes across, not on grammar or vocabulary.

## Limitations

Po aims to help users communicate more clearly, not change their natural voice or accent.

## Key Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Talk** | One recorded conversation, as Po lists it. |
| **Feedback** | What Po tells the user about a talk: what went well and what to try differently. |
| **Voice sample** | A short recording of the user speaking, made once during setup, so Po can recognize their voice. |
